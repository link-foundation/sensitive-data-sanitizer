Resolve the sanitizer requests of issues 130–141. Placeholder mode accepts `transformation.existing: 'keep'` for a second pass over sanitized text, types whole JSON values (`Sensitive-1`, `Age-1`), and keeps pronouns, cue words and sentence-initial greetings and verbs readable beside `Person-n`; compact organization and location spans become one typed token. `reservedPlaceholderTypes()` and `isPlaceholderToken()` expose the namespace. `strict` covers more nationality, religion, health, sexuality, conviction and trade-union wording, and `vocabulary.personalAttributes` adds caller stems. A credentials-only entry (`@link-foundation/sensitive-data-sanitizer/credentials`) starts about three times faster, and both entries support `require`. Credential context stays readable and masks stay stable; configuration settings are kept and an `exempt(finding, text)` hook can keep findings by policy. New detections cover CI credential markers, XML attributes, hash rockets, markup values and plural credential flags. Wrapped base64 and hex keep their layout; candidates are bounded per 64 KiB window; `knownSecrets` also match their hex and base64 spellings. Environment secret discovery selects `PGPASSWORD`-style names and cookies, skips more configuration names, and accepts `minLength`.
